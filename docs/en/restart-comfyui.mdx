---
title: "Restart ComfyUI"
description: "Restart ComfyUI in the Editor and on the Worker after installing a new Custom Node."
---

After installing a new Custom Node, restart ComfyUI in the Editor and on the Worker.

## Restart in the Editor (Local)

1. Select the Settings icon in the title bar.
2. Select `ComfyUI`.
3. Select `Restart`.

## Restart on the Worker (GPU Server)

When connected to a Worker, restart its ComfyUI as follows.

1. Select `B` in the title bar.
2. Select `Restart`.
